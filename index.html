Our Story

Jack and Caroline's story began during their senior year at Chapman University, though their paths had started much earlier in San Diego. Caroline attended Torrey Pines High School, while Jack went to Santa Fe Christian, but somehow they never crossed paths until college.

Their first meeting happened in the university parking structure. Caroline and her friends had gotten hold of a megaphone and were entertaining themselves by yelling at unsuspecting students. One of their favorite targets was a guy in a bro tank, whom Caroline loudly scolded with, "NO EATING ON THE SIDEWALK!" Moments later, Jack walked up because his car happened to be parked on the very same level. What started as a random encounter quickly turned into something much more.

Between fraternity date nights, Jack teaching Caroline how to drive his old stick-shift BMW, countless walks to class, and Jack's persistence, the two spent more and more time together. They fell in love quickly and effortlessly, discovering that the best stories sometimes begin with the most unexpected introductions—in this case, a parking garage and a megaphone.
